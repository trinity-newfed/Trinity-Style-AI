How to Measure

A. Shoulder Width Measure across the back from the tip of one shoulder bone to the other.
B. Chest / Bust Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape horizontal.
C. Natural Waist Measure around your narrowest waistline (typically where your body bends side to side).
D. Hips Stand with feet together and measure around the fullest part of your hips.
Fit Recommendation

TRINITY pieces are tailored with a relaxed, architectural fall. If you are between sizes, we recommend taking your true size for the intended silhouette.
